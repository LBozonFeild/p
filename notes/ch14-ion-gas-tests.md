# Chapter 14 — Ion tests and gas tests (Papers 1 and 2 only)

**Sources:** 5070 Papers 1 and 2, **2010–2025**. Anything that does not appear in those papers is omitted. Paper 3 not used.

Write **test + result**. `/` = alternatives that score.

---

## Anions

**Carbonate, CO₃²⁻**

- add **dilute acid**, then test for CO₂
- **effervescence**; gas turns **limewater milky** / cloudy / white ppt

**Chloride, Cl⁻**

- acidify with **dilute nitric acid**, then **aqueous silver nitrate** (lead(II) nitrate also credited)
- **white ppt**

**Bromide, Br⁻**

- acidify with **dilute nitric acid**, then **aqueous silver nitrate**
- **cream ppt**

**Iodide, I⁻**

- acidify with **dilute nitric acid**, then **aqueous silver nitrate** / **lead(II) nitrate**
- **yellow ppt**

**Nitrate, NO₃⁻**

- **aqueous sodium hydroxide** + **aluminium foil**, **warm** (Devarda’s alloy also credited)
- **ammonia** (damp / moist red litmus → **blue**)
- also credited: **brown-ring** — iron(II) sulfate then **concentrated sulfuric acid** → **brown ring**

**Sulfate, SO₄²⁻**

- acidify with **dilute nitric acid**, then **aqueous barium nitrate** (barium chloride also credited)
- **white ppt**

**Sulfite, SO₃²⁻**

- (dilute) acid → gas that **decolourises acidified potassium manganate(VII)** / **purple to colourless**

## Aqueous cations (NaOH and NH₃)

Write **colour of ppt** + **soluble / insoluble in excess**.

If NaOH is added all at once, Al³⁺ / Zn²⁺ ppt may **not be seen** (it dissolves in excess).

| cation | aqueous sodium hydroxide | aqueous ammonia |
|---|---|---|
| **Al³⁺** | **white ppt, soluble in excess**, colourless solution | **white ppt, insoluble in excess** |
| **NH₄⁺** | **ammonia on warming** (damp red litmus blue) | — |
| **Ca²⁺** | **white ppt, insoluble in excess** | **no ppt** |
| **Cr³⁺** | **green ppt, soluble in excess** | **green ppt, insoluble in excess** |
| **Cu²⁺** | **light blue ppt, insoluble in excess** | **light blue ppt, soluble in excess**, **dark blue solution** |
| **Fe²⁺** | **green ppt, insoluble in excess** | same |
| **Fe³⁺** | **red-brown ppt, insoluble in excess** | same |
| **Zn²⁺** | **white ppt, soluble in excess**, colourless solution | **white ppt, soluble in excess**, colourless solution |

**Al³⁺ / Ca²⁺ / Zn²⁺ (all white with NaOH)**

- Al³⁺: soluble in excess NaOH, **insoluble in excess NH₃**
- Ca²⁺: **insoluble in excess NaOH**, **no ppt with NH₃**
- Zn²⁺: **soluble in excess NaOH and NH₃**

## Gases

| gas | test and result |
|---|---|
| **ammonia, NH₃** | **damp / moist red litmus paper blue** |
| **carbon dioxide, CO₂** | **limewater milky** / cloudy / white ppt |
| **chlorine, Cl₂** | **bleaches** (damp) litmus |
| **hydrogen, H₂** | **‘pops’** / explodes / squeaks with a **lighted splint** |
| **oxygen, O₂** | **relights a glowing splint** |
| **sulfur dioxide, SO₂** | acidified KMnO₄ **purple to colourless**; also acidified potassium dichromate(VI) **orange to green** |

- H₂: **lighted** splint  O₂: **glowing** splint
- CO₂: name **limewater** and **milky**

| Ca²⁺ | **orange-red** |

## Short list

- CO₃²⁻: acid → CO₂, limewater milky
- Cl⁻: HNO₃ then AgNO₃ → white ppt
- Br⁻: HNO₃ then AgNO₃ → cream ppt
- I⁻: HNO₃ then AgNO₃ / Pb(NO₃)₂ → yellow ppt
- SO₄²⁻: HNO₃ then Ba(NO₃)₂ → white ppt
- NO₃⁻: NaOH + Al foil, warm → NH₃
- SO₃²⁻: acidified KMnO₄ purple to colourless
- Al³⁺: white ppt, soluble excess NaOH, insoluble excess NH₃
- Zn²⁺: white ppt, soluble excess NaOH **and** NH₃
- Ca²⁺: white ppt NaOH, insoluble in excess; no ppt NH₃
- Cu²⁺: light blue ppt; excess NH₃ → dark blue solution
- Fe²⁺: green ppt, insoluble in excess
- Fe³⁺: red-brown ppt, insoluble in excess
- Cr³⁺: green ppt, soluble excess NaOH, insoluble excess NH₃
- NH₄⁺: warm NaOH → NH₃, damp red litmus blue
- H₂ lighted splint pop / O₂ glowing splint relights
- CO₂ limewater milky / Cl₂ bleaches litmus / NH₃ damp red litmus blue
- SO₂ acidified KMnO₄ purple to colourless / dichromate orange to green
- flame: Na yellow / K lilac / Ca orange-red / Cu blue-green
