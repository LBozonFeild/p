A. QUESTION PAPERS WITHOUT A MARK SCHEME IN THE REPOSITORY
----------------------------------------------------------
The folder physics-5054 has no mark scheme for these ten question papers, so none of their questions is in the bank: a question
is never shown without its own official scheme and the scheme of another paper is never attached to it.
For the reconciliation every question was still given its syllabus code. Questions whose text is identical to a question of a
verified paper take the code of that question; the others were coded by reading the question (no scheme to check against).
