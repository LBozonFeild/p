8.3 Defects found by this inspection and corrected in the file described here
  * Marks of EITHER/OR questions. The header of 4110-4111 (May/June 2010, Paper 2 variant 1, Question 7) said
    "3 of 7 marks" and that of 4293-4295 (May/June 2021, variant 2, Question 8) "3 of 9 marks": the marks of both
    alternatives had been added. A candidate answers one alternative, so the questions carry 5 and 6 marks. These two
    questions and May/June 2019 variant 1 Question 8 (4252-4254) also lacked the remark "EITHER / OR question" in the
    header. Cause: alternatives were recognised only where the tracker has separate rows for the OR part. They are
    now recognised from the text of the question paper. Check C2 of bverify.py was written for this: it compares the
    marks in the header of every Paper 2 record with the marks printed on the paper (part 5).
  * Names of the parts in the header. Where the paper prints one mark for several sub-parts, e.g. one "[2]" after
    (a)(i) and (a)(ii), the header named the last sub-part only, "(a)(ii)". 21 records, mostly of the two papers of
    2004 (seen on 2882: "(a)(ii), (b), (c)(ii) (7 of 7 marks)" for a scheme that also marks (a)(i) and (c)(i)).
    The header now names every sub-part the mark covers: "(a)(i)-(ii), (b), (c)(i)-(ii)".
  * Record 4.2.2|w21|P2|V2|Q5 (3905-3907): the whole question is "5 EITHER ... OR ..."; the header now says
    "EITHER (a), EITHER (b)(ii)" instead of "(a), (b)(ii)".
  * The front page and the exclusions file said that page numbers and section headings are never inside a picture.
    That was not accurate (see 8.2); both texts were corrected and the exclusions file gives the counts.
  * A heading shown with the wrong scheme. The mark scheme of May/June 2017 variant 2 prints the heading "SECTION B"
    in capitals at the top of the page that follows the scheme of Question 8, the last question of Section A. The
    heading was shown as a strip of its own below the scheme of Question 8 (3710). Two more schemes print it in
    capitals (May/June 2004 and May/June 2014 variant 1); their Question 8 is not in the bank. The heading is now
    kept with the first question of Section B, as in all other schemes: 3710 shows the scheme of Question 8 only,
    and the scheme of Question 9 of the three papers begins with the heading (771, 1893, 2117, 2476, 2845, 2969,
    3246). No other picture of a scheme holds a heading that is not its first line (exclusions file, part D2).
