# 14 Coordination and control

## 14.1 Mammalian nervous system
**Coordinates / regulates** body functions: **brain + spinal cord + nerves**.

**CNS** = brain **and** spinal cord.  
**PNS** = nerves **outside** brain and spinal cord.

**ID neurones (diagrams):**  
- **sensory:** receptor → CNS; cell body in middle of axon / dorsal root.  
- **relay:** CNS only; links sensory to motor.  
- **motor:** CNS → **effector** (muscle or gland); cell body in CNS.

**Impulses** = **electrical**; travel **along neurones**.

**Reflex arc (order locked):** receptor → sensory neurone → relay neurone → motor neurone → **effector** (muscle **or** gland).  
**Reflex action** = **rapid** + **automatic** response to a stimulus (protective).

**Synapse** = **junction** between two neurones.  
**Structure:** vesicles with **neurotransmitter**; **synaptic gap**; **receptor proteins**.  
**Events:** impulse arrives → vesicles release neurotransmitter **into the gap** → **diffuse** across → **bind** receptor proteins → impulse in **next** neurone.  
Synapses → impulses **one direction only** (receptors only on postsynaptic side / vesicles only in first neurone).

**R:** blood carries the impulse; synapse = neurone; effector missing; brain **must** be in every reflex (spinal reflex does not need brain).

## 14.2 Sense organs
Sense organs = **groups of receptor cells** for **light, sound, touch, temperature, chemicals**.

**Eye ID (limited to):** cornea, iris, pupil, lens, ciliary muscles, suspensory ligaments, retina, fovea, optic nerve, blind spot.

| part | function (this wording scores) |
|---|---|
| cornea | **refracts** light |
| iris | controls **how much light** enters the **pupil** |
| lens | **focuses** light onto the **retina** |
| ciliary + suspensory | control **shape of the lens** |
| retina | **light receptors**; some sensitive to **different colours** |
| fovea | **greatest density** of light receptors |
| optic nerve | **impulses to the brain** |
| blind spot | **no** receptors; optic nerve leaves |

**Pupil reflex (antagonistic iris muscles)**  
Bright: **circular** contract, **radial** relax → pupil **smaller** (less light in; protects retina).  
Dim: circular relax, radial contract → pupil **larger**.  
Receptors in **retina** (not iris/pupil).

**Accommodation**  
**Near:** ciliary **contract** → suspensory **slack** → lens **fat / more curved** → **more** refraction.  
**Distant:** ciliary **relax** → suspensory **taut** → lens **thin / less curved** → **less** refraction.

**R:** ciliary changes pupil; lens “moves”; focusing on the **iris**; rods/cones names not required.

## 14.3 Hormones
**Hormone** = **chemical**, produced by a **gland**, carried by the **blood**, alters activity of **specific target organ(s)**.

| gland | hormone(s) |
|---|---|
| adrenal | adrenaline |
| pancreas | insulin **and** glucagon |
| pituitary | FSH **and** LH |
| testes | testosterone |
| ovaries | oestrogen **and** progesterone |

**Adrenaline:** ↑ **blood glucose**; ↑ **heart rate**. Situations: fear, shock, danger, exercise, “fight or flight”. Liver: glycogen → glucose.

**Nervous vs hormonal (only these two):**  
- speed: nervous **faster**  
- duration: hormonal **longer**

**R:** hormone travels along a neurone; insulin **raises** glucose; adrenaline from pancreas.

## 14.4 Homeostasis
**Homeostasis** = maintenance of a **constant internal environment**.

**Negative feedback:** change from a **set point** is detected → response that **reverses** the change **back to the set point**.

## 14.5 Temperature
**Skin ID:** hairs, hair **erector** muscles, **sweat glands**, receptors, sensory neurones, blood vessels, **fatty tissue**.

**Insulation:** fatty tissue / trapped air in hair → **reduces heat loss**.

**Detectors:** **skin temperature receptors** + **hypothalamus**. Hypothalamus **coordinates** the responses.

| process | how it keeps core T constant |
|---|---|
| **sweating** | water **evaporates**; heat taken from skin |
| **shivering** | muscle contraction → **heat released** |
| **erector contract** | hairs stand → thicker **trapped-air** layer → less heat loss |
| **vasodilation** of **arterioles** to skin capillaries | more blood to surface → **more heat loss** |
| **vasoconstriction** of those arterioles | less blood to surface → **less heat loss** |

**R:** capillaries dilate/constrict (it is **arterioles**); sweat **cools the air**; hairs “warm you” with no trapped-air link.

## 14.6 Blood glucose
Must control [glucose]: too high/low harms cells / respiration / water potential of blood.

**High glucose:** pancreas → **insulin** → liver (and muscle) take up glucose / convert to **glycogen**.  
**Low glucose:** pancreas → **glucagon** → liver **glycogen → glucose**.

**Type 1:** ↑ blood glucose; **glucose in urine**. Treatment: **insulin** (injection).  
**R:** glucagon lowers glucose; insulin from liver; Type 1 = “too much insulin”.

# 15 Coordination and response in plants

**Gravitropism:** grow **towards or away from gravity** (shoot **negative**; root **positive**).  
**Phototropism:** grow **towards or away from light** (shoot **positive**).

**Auxin in the shoot only (this list):**  
(a) made in the **shoot tip**  
(b) **spreads** from the tip  
(c) **unequal** distribution in **light** and **gravity**  
(d) **stimulates cell elongation**

Light from one side → more auxin on **shaded** side → shaded cells elongate more → shoot **bends towards light**.  
Shoot on its side → more auxin on **lower** side → lower cells elongate more → shoot bends **up**.

**Investigate** tropisms in **shoots and roots** (clinostat / unilateral light / seedlings on side; equal vs unequal light).

**R:** auxin **inhibits** shoot elongation; auxin made in the root as a required point; hydrotropism.

# MCQ traps (recent)
- Pupil receptors are in the **retina**, not iris/pupil.  
- Bright light: **circular** contract (not ciliary).  
- Near object: ciliary **contract**, lens **fat**. Distant: ciliary **relax**, lens **thin**.  
- Synapse: chemical then electrical in the **next** neurone; **one way**.  
- Insulin **lowers** blood glucose; glucagon **raises**. Adrenaline **raises** glucose **and** heart rate.  
- Type 1 sign = **glucose in urine** / raised blood glucose — not “no glucose in blood”.  
- Auxin: **stimulates** elongation in **shoots**; more on **shaded / lower** side.  
- Homeostasis ≠ “body temperature never changes”; it is **returned to a set point**.  
- Vasodilation/constriction = **arterioles** to skin, not capillaries themselves.
